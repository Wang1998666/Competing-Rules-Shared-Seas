Figure 5 phased methanol investment

The experiment uses four cumulative annualized cost caps on the 2024 network:
5%, 10%, 15%, and 20% of the full feasible-port cost pool. Investments already
made in a plan remain in that plan. The four extra rows labeled fresh solve
the terminal budget independently for comparison. Benefits in the output are
annual run rates in Mt CO2e per year. The stages have no specific calendar dates.

With the dependencies and Times New Roman installed, from the extracted root:

    python Figure5/staged_investment.py EF-R1/analysis Figure5/results
    python Figure5/draw_figure5.py EF-R1/analysis/results/archived_plan_closure.csv Figure5/results/staged_results.csv Figure5/Figure_05

shortfall components, and the static matrix used in the figure. The editable
