Figure S1. EU endpoint proxy. (a) Ports flagged as EU/EEA endpoints in the 2024 study network; symbol size increases with the number of distinct study corridors incident to each port. (b) Marine portions of native H3 resolution-5 proxy cells occurring on archived representative corridor paths, clipped to the Natural Earth 1:10 million ocean layer. Cells belonging solely to port buffers are omitted from this panel. Colors retain the assigned proxy weights. (c) Counts and shares of all distinct proxy cells by weight; the denominator is the global proxy layer, not the regional port sample. The figure does not delineate EU ETS jurisdiction.
